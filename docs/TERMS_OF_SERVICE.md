## 2. Description of the Service

ImageShrink Pro provides an online SaaS software solution designed to optimize, compress, resize, sanitize, and convert digital image files in individual and bulk batches. 

Key technical capabilities include:
* Support for major image formats: JPEG/JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, TIFF, BMP, HEIC/HEIF, SVG, and ICO.
* Three distinct compression modes:
  * **Lossless Mode:** True byte optimization preserving pixel values, transparency, and ICC/EXIF metadata intact.
  * **Visually Lossless Mode:** High-efficiency perceptually identical compression maximizing byte savings.
  * **Custom Mode:** User-configurable parameters including target quality sliders (1–100%) and resizing dimensions.
* Generation of real-time visual comparison analytics (SSIM and PSNR perceptual metrics).
* Automated bundling of bulk results into single downloadable ZIP archives.
* 24-hour ephemeral download retention windows.
