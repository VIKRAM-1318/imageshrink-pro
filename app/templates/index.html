1. Core Privacy Commitments

ImageShrink Pro ("we," "us," or "our") operates a high-performance bulk image optimization and format conversion SaaS platform. We maintain rigorous standards of data protection, ensuring your visual media remains confidential, securely processed, and strictly ephemeral.

  • Zero AI/Model Training: We will never use customer-uploaded images, EXIF metadata, or visual features to train, benchmark, fine-tune, or evaluate machine learning or generative AI models.
  • Strict Ephemeral Storage: Uploaded originals and optimized files are stored in tenant-isolated directories and permanently deleted within twenty-four (24) hours (JOB_TTL_SECONDS = 86400).
  • No Commercial Data Brokerage: We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your personal data, uploaded media, or browsing habits to advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

A. Digital Image Files

When you utilize our bulk optimization engine, you provide digital media files (up to 35 MB per image in batches up to 500 images) in supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, TIFF, BMP, HEIC/HEIF, SVG, and ICO.

  • Lossless Mode: Bit-for-bit pixel verification preserves original EXIF metadata, ICC color profiles, and color space tags completely intact.
  • Visual Lossless & Custom Modes: Non-essential camera tags (such as GPS geolocation and camera serial numbers) are stripped by default to protect privacy and minimize byte size, while essential orientation and color profile tags are preserved.

B. User Account Data (Optional)

For users who register for an ImageShrink Pro account or subscription, we collect:

  • Account details: Name, email address, and cryptographically salted password hashes (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with dynamic salts and 600,000 iterations). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Subscription tier and quota status (e.g., Free, Pro, or Business plan identifiers).

3. Digital Asset Lifecycle & Automated Deletion

We enforce a strict temporal lifecycle on all user files:

  • Ingestion: Received via encrypted TLS 1.3 streams into isolated temporary buffers.
  • Processing: Converted and compressed in volatile memory and partitioned filesystem storage within seconds.
  • Download Window: Made accessible via unguessable, cryptographically random UUID v4 download URLs for up to 24 hours.
  • Irrevocable Purge: Continuous automated cleanup sweeps permanently unlink and shred files after 24 hours. Once deleted, files cannot be recovered or reconstructed by anyone.

4. Zero AI/ML Training Guarantee

We provide an unconditional, irrevocable guarantee regarding Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML): Customer media is processed solely through deterministic mathematical compression algorithms. We do not train, fine-tune, or supply training datasets to any AI model or third-party laboratory.

5. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, you hold distinct rights:

  • Right of Access & Erasure: You can request full deletion or export of your account profile at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update inaccurate profile details directly from your dashboard.
